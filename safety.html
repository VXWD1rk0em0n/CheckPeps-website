Safety and scope

The limits are the product. This page states them without hedging.

Scope

In scope

  • Mapping stated goals to relevant peptide categories
  • Educational context on those categories, in plain language
  • Screening the health information you choose to share for topics that belong with a professional
  • Identifying conditions that should escalate out of education
  • Routing toward licensed professionals

Out of scope

  • Prescribing, or recommending that you take anything
  • Doses, dose ranges, and schedules
  • Injection technique
  • Reconstitution instructions
  • Stacking and protocol guidance
  • Anything that replaces a licensed medical professional

Escalation

What happens when a conversation escalates

Escalation is a defined state, not a mood. When a conversation meets an escalation condition, the educational track stops and CheckPeps points you toward a licensed professional instead.

Escalation means:

  • The educational answer is not continued in a shorter or softer form.
  • Rephrasing the question does not reopen it. The boundary is in the system, not in the wording.
  • CheckPeps states plainly that the topic needs a professional, rather than going quiet or changing the subject.

Claims

Claims CheckPeps does not make

Being clear about what is not being asserted matters as much as the feature list:

  • No claim of FDA approval, clearance, or registration.
  • No claim to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or condition.
  • No claim of clinical efficacy for any peptide or category.
  • No guarantee of safety, results, or outcomes of any kind.
  • No claim that CheckPeps can substitute for the judgement of a licensed professional who knows your situation.
