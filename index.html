Why the safety model is different

The boundaries are built in, not asked for

Most assistants are told to be careful. CheckPeps is built so the careful answer is the only one it can give.

Structural, not stylistic

Refusal to give dosing, technique, reconstitution, or stacking guidance is part of the system's design rather than a tone it tries to hold. It does not erode over a long conversation or under rephrasing.

Escalation is a defined state

Specific conditions move a conversation out of the educational track and toward a licensed professional. That transition is a designed behaviour, not a judgement call made in the moment.

Education stays separate from instruction

CheckPeps explains what a peptide category is and how it is discussed. It does not convert that explanation into a protocol you could follow.

The handoff is the destination

Routing you to a licensed professional is the intended end of a clinical question — not a fallback for when the assistant runs out of answers.

What this does not mean

A designed boundary is a description of how CheckPeps behaves. It is not a guarantee of any outcome, and it does not make CheckPeps a substitute for professional medical judgement. Decisions about your health belong with a licensed professional who knows your situation.

Scope

Exactly what is and is not on offer

What CheckPeps does

  • Maps the goals you describe to relevant peptide categories
  • Provides educational context on those categories in plain language
  • Screens the health information you choose to share for topics that belong with a professional
  • Identifies conditions that should escalate out of an educational conversation
  • Routes you toward licensed professionals for anything clinical

What CheckPeps does not do

  • Prescribe, or recommend that you take anything
  • Give doses, dose ranges, or schedules
  • Explain injection technique
  • Provide reconstitution instructions
  • Provide stacking or protocol guidance
  • Replace a licensed medical professional

These are product boundaries enforced by design, not fine print. Nothing CheckPeps produces is a diagnosis, a treatment plan, or medical advice.
