1. Scope and Applicability

This Policy applies to CheckPeps’ wellness/fitness monitoring application, connected devices, and related services (the “Service”). It is drafted to align with:

  • FTC Act § 5 (unfair/deceptive practices — governs accuracy of privacy representations)
  • COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act, 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506) — if the Service is not directed at children under 13, state so explicitly and block known child accounts
  • CAN-SPAM Act — for marketing communications
  • FCRA (Fair Credit Reporting Act) — only if outputs are ever used for eligibility decisions (employment, insurance, credit); flag to counsel if any B2B/employer wellness-program integration exists
  • GINA (Genetic Information Nondiscrimination Act) — relevant only if any employer-sponsored wellness program integration collects health/genetic data; confirm applicability
  • State comprehensive privacy laws (CCPA/CPRA, VCDPA, CPA, CTDPA, UCPA, and others) — referenced generally below; no comprehensive federal consumer privacy law currently exists, so state law governs most individual rights
  • State biometric privacy statutes (e.g., BIPA-type laws) if device data includes biometric identifiers (heart rate patterns, etc.) — confirm with counsel given your data types

HIPAA note

Based on your input, CheckPeps does not collect medical/health records as a HIPAA Covered Entity or Business Associate. If CheckPeps ever integrates with healthcare providers, insurers, or employer group health plans, HIPAA applicability must be re-assessed before that integration ships.

FDA note

If the Service makes any diagnostic, disease-prevention, or treatment claims, it may qualify as a medical device under FDA’s Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) framework. Keep marketing/UI language limited to general wellness claims unless FDA clearance is obtained.
