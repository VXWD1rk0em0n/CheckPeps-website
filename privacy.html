Privacy Policy

Draft Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how CheckPeps handles information when you visit this website or interact with our educational tools.

Status: complete draft, pending legal review · Version: 1.0-draft

1. Age Restriction (Strictly 21+)

CheckPeps is strictly intended for individuals who are twenty-one (21) years of age or older. We do not intentionally collect, solicit, or maintain personal data from anyone under the age of 21. If we discover that an individual under 21 has provided information, it will be deleted immediately.

2. Information We Collect

Currently, CheckPeps requires no user account, login, or personal profile. We do not ask for or store Protected Health Information (PHI), medical records, or prescriptions.

  • Automated Logs: Like most websites, our hosting servers (e.g., Vercel) automatically collect basic technical logs (IP address, browser type, referring pages).
  • Educational Queries: Any queries or interactions with our educational assistant are processed for informational responses and are not tied to personal medical identities.

3. How We Use Information

We use technical data solely to:

  • Maintain, secure, and improve the performance of our website.
  • Prevent abuse, prompt injection, scraping, or unauthorized access.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting and CDN services) that process data only as necessary to deliver the website. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your data.

5. Contact Us

For questions regarding this policy, contact us at: support@checkpeps.com.
