Data & Privacy Practices

Transparent Architecture

CheckPeps operates on a privacy-first, data-minimization model. Because peptide education involves sensitive wellness interests, our systems are intentionally designed to collect as little personal data as possible.

Status: draft framework · Version: 1.0 · Last updated: [INSERT DATE]

1. Core Principles

  • Minimal Collection: We only process what is strictly necessary to answer educational queries and maintain site stability.
  • No Health Record Creation: We do not build clinical profiles, store patient charts, or act as an electronic health record (EHR).
  • Strict 21+ Age Gate: Our tools are engineered strictly for adults aged 21 and older. We do not knowingly retain information from anyone under 21.
  • No Data Monetization: We do not sell, rent, or trade your inquiries or wellness data to advertisers or data brokers.

2. What We Explicitly Do NOT Collect

To avoid security and privacy risks, CheckPeps does not ask for or maintain:

  • Protected Health Information (PHI) under HIPAA
  • Official medical records or laboratory diagnostic files
  • Social Security Numbers or government identification
  • Credit card or payment credentials (the educational tool is free)
  • Prescriptions or pharmacy insurance details

3. How Queries & Interactions Are Handled

When you interact with the CheckPeps educational assistant or search tool:

  • Ephemeral Processing: Queries are evaluated in real-time to match educational peptide categories and safety context.
  • Safety Filtering: Inputs are scanned for prohibited safety triggers (e.g., requests for dosing calculations, injection protocols, or sourcing illicit compounds) and redirected to safety warnings.
  • De-Identification: Telemetry logs used to diagnose technical errors are separated from personal user identifiers.

4. Infrastructure & Technical Security

We implement defense-in-depth measures to protect our visitors:

  • Encryption in Transit: All connections are forced over modern HTTPS using TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Server-Side Isolation: Cloud environments and edge networks (hosted on Vercel) deploy automated DDoS mitigation and firewall rules.
  • Strict Access Controls: Internal administrative access follows the principle of least privilege—no developer or team member has arbitrary access to user query logs.

5. Third-Party Infrastructure

CheckPeps relies on reputable enterprise infrastructure providers to deliver its services:

  • Hosting & Edge Network: Vercel Inc. (manages web traffic, caching, and serverless compute).
  • Analytics & Error Monitoring: Aggregated, anonymized performance metrics that measure uptime and load speeds without tracking individuals across the web.

6. Your Rights & Data Requests

Because we do not mandate user accounts or store persistent personal dossiers, most visits leave no identifiable data trail behind. If you have previously submitted an inquiry or believe we hold any personal information, you may request confirmation or full deletion at any time.

7. Contact Our Privacy Team

If you have questions about these technical practices, or wish to submit a data inquiry, please contact us electronically:
