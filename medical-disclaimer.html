The boundary, in plain language

CheckPeps provides general educational information about peptide categories. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it is not a substitute for consultation with a licensed healthcare professional.

CheckPeps does not prescribe. It does not provide doses, dose ranges, or schedules. It does not explain injection technique. It does not provide reconstitution instructions. It does not provide stacking or protocol guidance. Decisions about your health belong with a licensed professional who knows your situation.

CheckPeps is not an emergency service. If you may be experiencing a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services.
