1. Scope and Applicability

This Policy applies to CheckPeps’ wellness/fitness monitoring application, connected tools, and related educational services (the “Service”). It is drafted to align with:

  • FTC Act § 5: Unfair/deceptive practices — governs accuracy of privacy representations.
  • COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act, 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506): The Service is strictly not directed to anyone under 21, and child accounts are strictly blocked.
  • CAN-SPAM Act: Standards governing marketing and informational communications.
  • FCRA (Fair Credit Reporting Act): Service outputs are not used for credit, employment, or insurance eligibility determinations.
  • GINA (Genetic Information Nondiscrimination Act): Relevant to wellness integrations and genetic/health data restrictions.
  • State Comprehensive Privacy Laws: Reference standards under CCPA/CPRA, VCDPA, CPA, CTDPA, UCPA, and others.
  • State Biometric Privacy Statutes: Protections regarding biometric identifiers and physiological telemetry data.

HIPAA & FDA Notes

HIPAA: CheckPeps does not collect medical/health records as a HIPAA Covered Entity or Business Associate. If integrations with healthcare providers or insurance plans occur, HIPAA applicability will be formally reviewed.

FDA: The Service does not make diagnostic, disease-prevention, or treatment claims and does not qualify as a medical device under FDA Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) frameworks. Language is strictly limited to general wellness and educational context.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide, maintain, and operate the educational service.
  • Generate wellness insights, trends, and automated scores.
  • Improve, tune, and secure underlying algorithmic models.
  • Provide customer support, fraud prevention, and security verification.
  • Deliver informational or marketing communications (with opt-out mechanisms).
  • Ensure legal compliance and enforcement of our Terms of Service.

4. Automated Decision-Making & AI Disclosure

CheckPeps uses automated models and machine learning to generate outputs (e.g., wellness indicators, informational mapping, category suggestions) without manual human review prior to presentation.

  • Logic Involved: Generalized inputs (metrics provided by you, goal selections, usage patterns). No proprietary core model weights are shared.
  • Consequence: All outputs are for self-informational and educational purposes only. They are not used to make adverse decisions regarding credit, healthcare, or employment.
  • User Controls: Users have the right to request human review or dispute automated profiling where required under applicable state laws.

5. Legal Basis & Consent Model

Consent is obtained through:

  • Clickwrap acceptance of this Policy and the Terms of Service.
  • Operating-system level granular permission prompts (sensors, device storage).
  • Opt-in mechanisms for any direct marketing communications.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell wellness data. We may share data only with:

  • Service Providers & Processors: Cloud hosting, telemetry, and analytics under strict confidentiality contracts.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by subpoena, court order, or applicable law to protect life and safety.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with any merger, sale, or transfer of company assets.

Data is retained for [X months/years] or until account deletion is requested, except where longer retention is mandated for legal, compliance, or security purposes.

8. Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of wellness data, including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, principle-of-least-privilege access controls, and active incident monitoring. No system is 100% immune to vulnerabilities.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction and state of residence, you may possess the right to:

  • Access the specific pieces of personal data we maintain about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Opt out of automated profiling or request human review.
  • Request data portability.

10. Age Restriction & Minors (Strictly 21+)

CheckPeps is strictly restricted to individuals twenty-one (21) years of age or older. We do not knowingly permit access to, or collect data from, anyone under 21. In compliance with COPPA and youth protection regulations, if we learn that any user is under 21, their account and associated data will be expunged immediately.

11. International Users

The Service is hosted and operated in the United States. If you access the Service from other jurisdictions, your information will be transferred to and processed within the United States.

12. Changes to this Policy

Material updates will be accompanied by prominent notification on the site or via direct communication at least 30 days prior to taking effect, updating the effective date above.
