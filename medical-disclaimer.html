Medical Disclaimer

Not Medical Advice — Educational Use Only

CheckPeps is an educational technology tool. All content, assistant responses, category mappings, and data published on this website are provided strictly for general informational and educational purposes. Nothing on this platform is medical advice, diagnosis, or clinical guidance.

Emergency Notice

If you think you are having a medical emergency, adverse drug reaction, or life-threatening event, immediately call your local emergency number (such as 911 in the United States) or go to the nearest hospital emergency room. Never rely on CheckPeps in an emergency situation.

1. No Doctor-Patient Relationship

Using CheckPeps, querying the educational assistant, or reviewing educational peptide categories does not establish a physician-patient, pharmacist-patient, or healthcare provider relationship of any kind.

No confidential clinician-patient privilege is formed. Content generated by CheckPeps cannot substitute for a formal clinical evaluation with a qualified physician or endocrinologist.

2. Strict Scope Boundaries (What CheckPeps Will NEVER Provide)

CheckPeps is engineered with hardcoded safety boundaries. Under no circumstances will our service or automated tools:

  • Calculate, recommend, or verify dosages or dosing schedules.
  • Provide instructions for reconstitution, dilution, or solvent preparation.
  • Instruct on injection techniques, needle handling, or administration routes.
  • Design “stacks,” cycles, or polypharmacy combinations.
  • Sell, broker, ship, compound, or facilitate the purchase of any peptide or pharmaceutical substance.
  • Diagnose diseases, interpret laboratory bloodwork, or prescribe therapies.

3. Regulatory & Research Status of Peptides

Many peptides discussed or indexed across peptide literature are investigational, experimental, or unapproved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) or the European Medicines Agency (EMA) for human clinical use.

  • A significant number of compounds sold online are labeled strictly for “in-vitro research use only” and are not intended, formulated, or verified for human consumption.
  • CheckPeps makes no representations, claims, or warranties regarding the safety, purity, sterility, or legality of compounds offered by third-party research chemical vendors or compounding pharmacies.
  • The legal status of specific peptides varies significantly by state, territory, and country. You are solely responsible for knowing and adhering to the laws of your jurisdiction.

4. Consultation with Licensed Healthcare Providers

Always consult a licensed medical professional (such as an MD, DO, or licensed clinical specialist) before considering, modifying, or administering any peptide, prescription drug, or health regimen. Never delay or disregard professional medical advice because of something you read on CheckPeps.

5. Age Restriction (Strictly 21+)

CheckPeps and all related materials are strictly restricted to adults twenty-one (21) years of age or older. We do not knowingly permit use by, or direct content toward, individuals under 21.
